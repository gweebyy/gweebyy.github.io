Chocolate Chip Cookies

Just a simple chocolate cookie recipe. If you wanna make this super good, use browned butter.

Browned butter is legit just butter melted in a pan and keeping it on the heat until it has become brown looking and it has a caramel flavor.

If you do browned butter, keep an eye on it, because it suddenly bubbles and browns QUICKLY.

I'm pretty sure all recipes online are the same.

Ingredients Notes
1 and 1/2 cup all purpose flour Try being on the lesser side of the flour if possible
1 tspn baking soda
1/4 tspn salt
3/4 cup chocolate chips Chocolate chunks are also yummy
1 stick of butter Browned butter is also yummy
1/2 cup white sugar
1/4 cup brown sugar
1 tspn vanilla I always end up eyeballing this tbh
1 egg

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350 F, will be baking these for about 12 minutes

Microwave the butter to melt it for about 40 seconds. Don't let it get too hot or it will scramble the egg

Add in the white sugar, brown sugar, egg, and vanilla. Mix these until creamy looking.

Add in the flour, baking soda, salt, and chocolate chips. Mix these and it should be dough-y looking.

Form the dough into balls. Big balls. Little balls. Whatever. Bake it in the oven for like 12 minutes or until done looking.

Let them chill for a while outside the oven or you may burn your tongue.
