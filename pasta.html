Pasta

This is my pasta recipe. I do it all in one pot, because I hate using a lot of dishes.

It's like a template almost for how I cook my pasta. I just use jarred marinara or vodka sauce.

At the very, very least. You just need the pasta, sauce, and water to cook the pasta. The protein, vegetables, and seasonings are optional. However, they make me happier having it.

I like pasta. It comes together so fast and easy. The way I do it, there is no need to strain the pasta.

If you want to cook the pasta by itself and strain it and everything. Go ahead. I legit can't stop you

This is good enough for about 4 or 5 portions.

Ingredients Notes
16 oz pasta
1 jar of pasta Save the jar for water
1 jar of water
protein Ex. sausage, ground beef, chicken
vegetables Ex. onions, bell pepper, garlic
seasonings Ex. black pepper, salt, paprika, garlic powder

Instructions

Get out a pot and put the stove on medium-high heat.

Fully cook your choice of protein. I like cooking the protein in the beginning, so that it browns well

Add in your choice of vegetables at this point. I like having at least some onion, bell pepper, and spinach.

Add in your choice of seasonings. I like adding at least ground black pepper, salt, garlic powder, and paprika. If I have it, I'll also add hot sauce and chicken bouillon.

After cooking all that for a few minutes, add in the sauce.

Reuse the sauce jar and fill it up with water and add it into the pot. I like shaking it up to get the leftover sauce.

Mix all that together until it is nice and even looking, then add the whole box of pasta.

Mix it all together again until it is even and the liquid should cover all of the pasta.

Cover this with a lid and let it come to a bubbling boil. Stir every like 3 minutes. Make sure to scrape the bottom of the pot, so that nothing burns on the bottom.

The pasta should be cooked after about 10 minutes. If it isn't done enough, just let it cook some more.

You might have to add more a little more water into the pot if the pasta isn't cooked enough.

Once the pasta is all cooked to your liking, take the pot lid off and let the sauce boil down so that it is thicker

If you like your sauce soupy, you can skip that. If not, then just let the water boil and evaporate down.
